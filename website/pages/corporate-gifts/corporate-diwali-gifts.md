## Who companies gift at Diwali

- **Employees who celebrate Diwali**, as recognition of an important festival
- **Whole teams**, as part of an inclusive celebration of the festival
- **Clients and partners**, especially in relationships connected to India
- **Workplace celebrations** organized by employee resource groups, with décor and favors for the event

## Gift ideas sourced from India

- **Diyas and tea-light holders:** decorated clay, brass, or thread-wrapped holders like those pictured
- **Torans and wall hangings** for doorways and offices
- **Brass and copper pieces**, such as small bowls, urlis, and lamps
- **Textiles:** stoles, cushion covers, and table runners
- **Gift bags and boxes:** [jute or cotton bags](/custom-manufacturing/custom-jute-bags) or handcrafted boxes that are part of the gift
- **Rangoli kits** for office celebrations

Which items are available, in what quantities, and with what customization depends on the suppliers for your program.

## A note on food gifts

Sweets and dry fruits are traditional Diwali gifts, but imported food has its own US requirements. A common approach is to source a non-food gift from India and pair it with sweets from a local or US vendor.

## Choosing gifts thoughtfully

- **Consider the whole audience.** Not everyone celebrates Diwali, and many companies offer the gift or the event to all colleagues.
- **Avoid leather and alcohol** for recipients who may not use them; some Hindus and Jains avoid leather, and many people don't drink.
- **If you include food, keep it vegetarian.**
- **Keep branding subtle.** A tag, card, or branded bag often works better than a large logo on a decorative piece.

## Branding and packaging

- **Custom gift boxes** with your logo
- **Branded gift bags** in jute or cotton
- **Printed sleeves, tags, and message cards**
- **Per-recipient personalization**, such as names, which you can ask about

## Timing your program

Diwali follows the Hindu lunar calendar and falls in October or November, on a different date each year. Gifts made in India need time for sourcing, production, shipping, and customs, so work backward from your delivery date and finalize quantities early.

## Delivery to one office or many addresses

Bulk delivery to a single office is the simplest. Delivery to individual employees' homes adds packing and shipping work. Tell us which you need in your request; options and costs are confirmed in your quote.

## What to include in your request

- **Number of recipients**, and any groups receiving different gifts
- **Budget per gift**
- **Gift ideas**, or the feeling you want the gift to convey
- **Branding:** logo files and where they should appear
- **Delivery:** one address or many, and the cities or states involved
- **Delivery date**, or the date of your celebration

## How buying through India Source Co. works

- **You buy from a US company.** India Source Co. is operated by Miraaya LLC. Suppliers in India invoice Miraaya LLC, and Miraaya LLC invoices your business. You get one quote, one invoice, and one point of contact.
- **You receive a quotation from India Source Co.** for the requested products. Payment terms are stated in each quote.
- **We manage the supplier relationship.** That includes clarifying specifications, arranging samples where a supplier offers them, following production, and keeping you informed.

## What we look at before quoting

Before we commit to a quote, we check whether a request makes sense for everyone involved:

- **Completeness:** whether the specifications are detailed enough for suppliers to price accurately
- **Feasibility:** whether suppliers we can work with make this kind of product, with these materials and methods
- **Quantity:** whether the quantity is practical for the type of production involved
- **Requirements:** labeling, testing, or other rules that apply to the product in the US
- **Landed cost:** what the product is likely to cost once freight, duties, and other import costs are included
- **Timing:** whether your target date leaves enough time for sampling, production, and shipping

If a request isn't viable — for example, the quantity is too small for any supplier we can find, the target price can't be met, or the product needs something we can't arrange — we'll tell you.

## What to include in a request

Suppliers can only price what they can see. The most useful requests include:

- **The product**, with photos, drawings, or a sample description
- **Materials** and any you want to avoid
- **Dimensions or sizes**, with tolerances if they matter
- **Customization:** logo files, print or embroidery method, colors, labels, and packaging
- **Quantity**, or a range, broken down by size or color if relevant
- **Destination:** city and state, and whether everything ships to one address
- **Target date** for delivery
- **Target budget or unit price**, if you have one
- **Requirements** such as certifications, testing, or labeling your customers expect

## Import, duties, and product requirements

Imported products must meet US requirements that vary by product. Depending on what you're buying, these can include country-of-origin marking, textile fiber and care labeling, testing and certification for children's products, and state rules such as California's Proposition 65. Duty rates depend on each product's tariff classification.

Who acts as importer of record, how customs brokerage and duties are handled, and which requirements apply to your product are confirmed for each order in your quote.

## What we don't do

- We don't own factories or claim capabilities on a supplier's behalf.
- We don't publish price lists, fixed minimums, or standard lead times, because they depend on the product and the supplier.
- We don't claim certifications for suppliers. If your project requires one, such as a social-compliance audit or organic certification, include it in your request and we'll tell you whether a supplier we can work with holds it.

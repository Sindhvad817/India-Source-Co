## What private label can include

- **Your brand:** woven or printed neck labels, hang tags, and packaging with your name
- **Your designs:** garments made from your sketches or tech packs
- **Adapted styles:** traditional silhouettes made in your choice of fabrics, colors, and embellishment, where a supplier offers that
- **Your presentation:** folding, polybags, and packaging that's ready for your shelves or warehouse

## Categories you can request

- **Women's:** kurta sets, salwar suits, anarkalis, sharara and gharara sets, lehengas, sarees with blouses, and dupattas
- **Men's:** kurtas, kurta pajama sets, Nehru jackets, and sherwanis
- **Kids':** festive sets for girls and boys
- **Fusion:** Indo-western and contemporary cuts

Whether a particular style can be produced, and in what quantity, depends on the suppliers available for it.

## What to put in a tech pack

A tech pack is the document suppliers quote and produce from. The clearer it is, the closer the first sample will be.

- **Flat sketches** of the front and back, with construction notes
- **A measurement chart** for every size, with grading between sizes
- **Fabric:** type, weight, and fiber content, plus lining
- **Trims:** buttons, tassels, lace, piping, and zips
- **Embellishment:** embroidery or print artwork with size and placement
- **Colorways:** color references for each, ideally physical swatches
- **Labels:** brand, size, care, fiber content, and country of origin
- **Packaging:** folding, polybags, hang tags, and barcodes
- **Quantities** by style, color, and size

## Sizing for US customers

Indian sizing often follows chest or bust measurements in inches with S–XXL labels, and fits vary between suppliers. Give garment measurements for each size and the ease you want. For sarees and fitted blouses, many boutiques offer unstitched or semi-stitched blouse pieces so customers can have them tailored.

## Planning around the festival calendar

Demand for ethnic wear rises around Diwali, Navratri, Eid, and wedding season. Work backward from your launch date and leave time for sampling and revisions, production, shipping, and customs. Include your launch date in your request so we can tell you whether it's realistic.

## Labeling and compliance for apparel

Apparel imported for sale in the US must carry labels showing fiber content, country of origin, and the manufacturer or dealer (often an RN number), plus care instructions under the FTC's labeling rules. Children's clothing has additional testing and certification requirements under the Consumer Product Safety Improvement Act. Which requirements apply to your collection, and how they'll be handled, is confirmed in your quote.

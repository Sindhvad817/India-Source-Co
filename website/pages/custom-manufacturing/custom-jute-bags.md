## Choosing the material

- **100% jute (hessian or burlap):** a coarse, natural texture and the classic rustic look. Natural color varies slightly from batch to batch.
- **Juco (jute and cotton blend):** a softer, smoother surface than pure jute, which often holds more detailed printing.
- **Laminated jute:** a thin inner coating, usually polypropylene, that adds stiffness and moisture resistance and helps a bag stand upright. It also makes the bag less straightforward to recycle or compost.
- **Natural or dyed:** jute can be dyed, but deep or bright colors look different on its texture than on cotton.

Suppliers usually describe fabric weight in grams per square meter (GSM). Ask for it in every quote so you can compare like with like.

## Construction details to specify

- **Dimensions:** width × height × gusset (the depth of the base and sides). A gusset makes room for bottles, boxes, and folded clothing.
- **Handles:** jute rope, cotton webbing, padded or cushioned handles, or bamboo and cane. State the handle length you want, since short handles are for hand-carrying and longer ones go over the shoulder.
- **Closures:** open top, button and loop, snap, or zipper.
- **Extras:** inside or outside pockets, a cotton lining, a stiffened base board, or bottle dividers for wine bags.
- **Load:** if the bags will carry groceries or heavy items, say so, so reinforcement at the handle joints is considered.

## Printing and branding

- **Screen printing** suits bold logos in one to a few solid colors. Each color usually adds a separate screen and setup.
- **Heat transfer and DTF** suit multi-color artwork and gradients, though the finish sits on the surface of the fabric.
- **Embroidery** gives a premium, raised look for smaller logos.
- **Woven labels, leather-look or fabric patches, and hang tags** add branding without printing on the bag itself.

Jute's open weave limits fine detail. Keep thin lines and small text larger than you would on paper, or choose juco or laminated jute for crisper results. Send vector artwork (AI, EPS, SVG, or a high-resolution PDF) and note where the print should go and how large it should be.

## Common uses

- **Retail and grocery bags** that customers reuse
- **Trade shows and conferences**, as attendee bags
- **Wedding welcome bags** for hotel guests
- **Wine and bottle bags** for wineries and gift shops
- **Gift packaging** for hampers and corporate gifts

## What to include in your request

- **Use:** what the bag is for and what goes inside
- **Material:** jute, juco, or laminated, and natural or dyed
- **Size:** width, height, and gusset, or a photo of a bag with similar proportions
- **Handles, closures, and extras**
- **Branding:** artwork files, number of colors, method, and placement
- **Packaging:** bulk-packed, or individually folded and bagged
- **Quantity**, destination, and target date
- **Target unit price**, if you have one

## Labeling and import considerations

Imported bags generally need country-of-origin marking, and the costs of freight, duties, and customs brokerage add to the price of each bag. Requirements and costs depend on the product. We confirm what applies to your order, and what your quote includes, before anything is made.

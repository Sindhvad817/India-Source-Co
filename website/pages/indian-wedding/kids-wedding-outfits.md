## Comfort comes first

A wedding day can mean hours of ceremonies, sitting on the floor, a long dinner, and late-night dancing. The outfits children are happiest in tend to share a few traits:

- **Soft linings** wherever embroidery, sequins, or metallic zari would otherwise touch the skin
- **Breathable fabrics** such as cotton silk, chanderi, or soft georgette, especially for summer or outdoor events
- **Easy closures:** elastic or drawstring waists and simple fastenings, so toddlers don't need help every time
- **Sensible lengths** that won't trip them up on stairs or the dance floor
- **Lighter embellishment** for the youngest children, keeping the heavier pieces for photos

Linings, fabric choices, and closures depend on the outfit and the maker. Tell us which of these matter most and we'll confirm what's possible before production.

## Popular styles for girls

- **Lehenga choli:** a flared skirt with a blouse and a light dupatta, the classic choice for weddings
- **Sharara or gharara:** wide, flowing pants with a kurta, easy to move and dance in
- **Anarkali:** a long, flared kurta that feels like a dress and needs no separate skirt
- **Pattu pavadai:** a traditional South Indian silk skirt and blouse
- **Kurti with a skirt or palazzo**, like the outfit pictured, for a festive look that stays light

## Popular styles for boys

- **Kurta pajama**, sometimes with a short Nehru jacket, the most comfortable option
- **Sherwani sets**, a smaller version of the groom's look for ring bearers and close family
- **Bandhgala or jodhpuri**, a structured, formal jacket for receptions
- **Dhoti kurta**, traditional for many ceremonies

## Sizing children who are still growing

Children can change sizes in a matter of months. Measure as close to production as you can, note each child's age and height alongside their measurements, and tell us the event date so we understand how much time there is. If you're ordering well in advance, say so; we'll explain what options exist for the outfit you choose.

## Matching the family

Coordinated family outfits photograph beautifully and are a common request. The approaches range from **identical fabric** across the whole family, to **mini versions** of the parents' styles, to a **shared color palette** where everyone wears something different. Send us the adults' outfits or colors along with the children's details so everything is planned together. Whether identical fabric is available in the quantity you need depends on the supplier, and we'll confirm it before you commit. For the adults, see our guides to [custom lehengas](/custom-indian-clothing/custom-lehenga) and [custom sherwanis](/custom-indian-clothing/custom-sherwani).

## What to include in your request

- **Each child's age** and size or measurements
- **The events** each child will attend, and the date
- **Inspiration photos** and colors
- **What they should match**, if anything
- **Comfort needs**, such as sensitive skin or a preference for no heavy embellishment
- **A budget range**

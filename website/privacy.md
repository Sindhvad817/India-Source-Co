This policy explains what information we collect through indiasourceco.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. India Source Co. is operated by Miraaya LLC, 213 Longstone Drive, Cherry Hill, NJ 08003, USA. Questions about this policy can be sent to [hello@indiasourceco.com](mailto:hello@indiasourceco.com).

## Information you give us

We collect the information you choose to send us:

- **Custom Request form:** your name, email address, and, if you provide them, your phone number, a description of what you're looking for, quantity, budget, timeline, and any photos you upload.
- **Contact form:** your name, email address, and message.
- **Email:** anything you send to us directly.

Please don't include sensitive information such as payment card numbers, government ID numbers, or health information in a form or email. If you're asking about children's clothing, details like age and size are all we need.

## Information included automatically when you submit a form

So we know which pages lead to inquiries, each form submission also includes:

- the page you sent the form from, and the product category of that page;
- the first page you visited on our site;
- the web address of the site that referred you on that first visit, without any search terms or other details that follow a "?" in the address;
- any campaign tags (known as UTM parameters, such as `utm_source`) that were in the link you first used to reach our site.

This information is sent only when you submit a form.

## What our site stores in your browser

To include the "first visit" details above, our site saves one item in your browser's local storage, named `isc_first_touch_v1`. It contains the first page you visited, the referring site's address, any campaign tags, and the time of that visit.

- It stays on your device and is sent to us only as part of a form you choose to submit.
- After 90 days it is treated as expired and replaced with details from your next visit.
- You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Our website does not set cookies.

## Analytics and advertising

We do not currently use analytics or advertising services such as Google Analytics, and we do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. If that changes, we will update this policy.

## How forms are processed

Our forms are delivered through **Web3Forms**, a form-processing service. When you submit a form, your browser sends the information, including any photos you attach, to Web3Forms, which forwards it to our email inbox. Web3Forms handles this information under its own privacy policy, available at [web3forms.com](https://web3forms.com).

## Website hosting

Our website is hosted by **Vercel**. To deliver and protect the site, Vercel processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. Vercel's privacy policy is available at [vercel.com](https://vercel.com).

Our pages do not load third-party analytics, advertising, or font services. The site's fonts are served from our own website.

## Links to other sites

Our site links to other websites, including our sister brand Miraaya Designs on Etsy, Instagram, and Facebook. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which apply when you visit them.

## How we use your information

We use the information you send us to:

- respond to your inquiry;
- look for options and prepare quotes;
- coordinate any order you decide to place;
- understand which pages and sources lead to inquiries, so we can improve our website.

We do not sell your personal information.

## When we share information

To find options or fulfill an order, we may share the details of your request, such as descriptions, photos, sizes, and quantities, with designers, artisans, and manufacturers, primarily in India. We share your contact details with them only when that's needed to complete an order you've approved.

We also rely on service providers that process information on our behalf, such as Web3Forms, Vercel, and our email provider. We may disclose information when required by law.

## How long we keep information

We keep inquiry emails and related records for as long as we need them to respond to you, prepare quotes, complete orders, and maintain our business records, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.

## Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you've sent us by emailing [hello@indiasourceco.com](mailto:hello@indiasourceco.com). You can also clear the browser storage described above at any time.

## Children's privacy

Our website is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parents and guardians requesting children's clothing should share only details like age and size.

## Security and location

Information you send through our website is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. Because we work with suppliers in India and use service providers that may operate in several countries, your information may be processed outside the country where you live.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
